# Product Context: Financial Fraud Graph & Transaction Anomaly Workbench

## Product Definition
An investigation workbench and decision-support terminal for financial fraud intelligence teams, anti-money laundering (AML) investigators, and risk analysts discovering synthetic rings, cyclic funds routing, mule networks, and transaction anomalies.

## Primary Audience & Real-World Scene
- **Audience**: Senior fraud analysts, AML forensic investigators, and risk operations officers conducting deep-dive 40-60 minute investigation sessions.
- **Environment**: High-density desktop displays (1440px+), multi-tab investigation flows, evidence gathering for legal, regulatory, and SAR (Suspicious Activity Report) filings.
- **Key User Jobs**:
  1. Ingest raw and enriched banking/UPI batches, validate mappings, and preserve record provenance.
  2. Inspect high-risk rule & ML alerts with complete explainability (score breakdown, graph structural evidence, raw payloads).
  3. Explore suspicious counterparty neighbourhoods in Cytoscape up to 2 hops without information overload.
  4. Aggregate findings into cases, manage team tasks, annotate audit trails, and formalize human-led fraud decisions.
  5. Calibrate detector rules and monitor false-positive feedback trends.

## Experience & Visual Principles
- **Mode**: Operate (Mission-critical analyst tool, density, predictability, strict semantic accuracy).
- **Tone**: Calm, precise, evidential, authoritative.
- **Visual Stance**: Grounded in the warm paper-slate aesthetic of `Design.png`: warm off-white canvas (`#f5f3ec`), deep navy/slate analytical consoles (`#121927`), boxed brutalist type badges, tabular numerals, unambiguous risk signals.
- **Human Authority**: The machine proposes scores and patterns; human investigators decide and record rationale.
